Research

Learning across devices.
Protecting data.

My work connects federated learning, edge intelligence, privacy preservation, and AI for security.

01 / FEDERATED LEARNING

Efficient learning across distributed systems

My doctoral research focused on privacy-aware, efficient multi-tier federated learning for mobile IoT systems. Related work includes client clustering in Mobile_FL, communication-efficient training in ComFLEX, and a survey of incentivized federated learning.

02 / PRIVACY & SECURITY

Understanding risks in collaborative AI

My publications examine adversarial attacks on federated learning, privacy-preserving analysis of Internet of Medical Things data, and federated intrusion detection for Android systems.

03 / HEALTHCARE AI

From distributed health data to detection

At Nottingham Trent University, I work on lung cancer detection using CPRD electronic health record data through the HORIZON Europe Phase IV AI project.

04 / EDGE INTELLIGENCE & HCI

AI closer to people and devices

As a Doctoral Fellow at the iHub–HCI Foundation at IIT Mandi, I explored the applicability of federated learning to HCI applications. My visiting research at the University of Agder contributed to the Indo-Norwegian collaboration in autonomous cyber-physical systems.
