Collaborative learning.
Privacy-aware intelligence.

I study federated learning and edge intelligence, with a focus on privacy, efficiency, and security.

I am a Research Fellow at Nottingham Trent University, working on lung cancer detection using electronic health records through the HORIZON Europe Phase IV AI project, under the guidance of Prof. David Brown.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at IIIT Kottayam in 2024, advised by Dr. Jayakrushna Sahoo. My doctoral research explored privacy-aware, efficient multi-tier federated learning for mobile IoT systems.
